Microsoft has set an end-of-support date for the Regression Suite Automation Tool (RSAT). After May 15, 2027, Microsoft will stop shipping bug fixes, security patches, and feature updates for RSAT. For teams looking to advance their testing for Dynamics 365, this change presents an opportunity to incorporate AI and agents into their workflows.
